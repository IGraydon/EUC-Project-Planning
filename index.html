External Support Options - Decision Required

Context: Internal skills assessment review confirms our in-house team lacks the capacity, experience, and specific technical skills required to deliver a migration project of this scale and complexity. We require external specialist support across multiple technical domains including M365 migration, CRM migration, business application matching/upgrading/replacement, observability migration, IAM migration, and data migration.

Option 1: Full Service Outsource Partner

Model: Single outsource partner handles the majority of technical delivery tasks. Internal team focuses on PMO, business liaison, and BAU continuity. IT Carve-Out Lead manages the partner via SLAs and maintains overall programme control.

PROS:
  • Single point of accountability for delivery
  • Experienced team familiar with carve-out complexity
  • Faster mobilization - one contract, one onboarding
  • Reduced internal coordination overhead
  • Partner owns integration between workstreams
  • Clear escalation path and governance model
  • Internal team can focus on BAU and business engagement
CONS:
  • Higher total cost - premium for integrated service
  • Less internal knowledge retention
  • Vendor lock-in for duration of programme
  • May lack niche specialist depth in some areas
  • Potential for scope creep in charging model
  • Limited internal team skill development
  • Dependency risk if partner underperforms

Option 2: Multiple Specialist Sub-Project Partners

Model: Separate specialist companies for each major sub-project (M365, CRM, Business Applications, Observability, IAM, Data). Each partner owns their specific workstream. Internal PMO coordinates across all partners. IT Carve-Out Lead manages all partners and owns integration points.

PROS:
  • Best-in-class specialists for each technical domain
  • Competitive pricing - can tender each workstream separately
  • Greater flexibility to scale up/down per workstream
  • Reduced dependency risk - failure of one partner doesn't impact others
  • Easier to replace underperforming partner in specific area
  • Access to deep niche expertise (e.g., specific CRM platform)
CONS:
  • High coordination overhead - multiple contracts, invoices, governance meetings
  • Integration risk - dependencies between partners difficult to manage
  • Finger-pointing risk when issues span multiple workstreams
  • Slower mobilization - multiple procurements and onboardings
  • Internal PMO capacity stretched managing multiple vendors
  • Conflicts in approach/methodology between different partners
  • Requires strong internal technical leadership to orchestrate

Option 3: Specialist Contractors (Augmentation Model)

Model: Individual specialist contractors embedded within internal team across key technical areas (M365, CRM, Business Applications, IAM, Data, etc.). Contractors work alongside internal staff under direct management of IT Carve-Out Lead. Internal team retains full delivery ownership and control.

PROS:
  • Maximum internal knowledge retention and skill development
  • Full control over methodology, approach, and daily execution
  • Most cost-effective option - paying for FTE time only
  • Flexible - can scale contractor numbers up/down as needed
  • Seamless integration with internal team culture and ways of working
  • Easier to pivot approach mid-programme
  • Builds long-term internal capability for future projects
CONS:
  • Highest risk - delivery accountability remains with internal team
  • Requires strong internal project management and technical leadership
  • No SLA or performance guarantees from contractors
  • Quality of contractors variable - hiring risk
  • Internal team must manage all integration, dependencies, issues
  • Longer procurement - individual contractor assessments and selection

Executive Summary

Scope: End User Computing (EUC) migration for 480 acquired users from seller infrastructure to corporate IT platform within 12-month TSA period. Users will retain seller-provided laptops and software throughout TSA period.

Approach: Two-phase migration strategy. Phase 1: Deploy dual access solution (VDI/Web Access/ANO) enabling users to access corporate M365 and critical SaaS tools while still using seller laptops (Q2-Q3 2026). Phase 2: Physical hardware migration in waves, transitioning users to corporate devices (Q4 2026). T-shirt sizing (5 user personas: XS, S, M, L, XL) enables bulk provisioning without detailed individual user data.

Current Status: Current State Assessment underway (started 26-Jan). User type/role data request to seller planned for 2-Feb (expected response mid-Feb). MAJOR BLOCKER: Seller must agree to a security/IAM solution to enable dual access - IT Carve-Out Lead leading negotiations. CRITICAL DECISION REQUIRED: Select external support delivery model (Option 1: Full Outsource / Option 2: Specialist Partners / Option 3: Contractors) by mid-Feb - internal skills assessment confirms we lack capacity, experience, and key technical skills to deliver this scale of migration independently.

Next 90 Days: Request seller user type data (2-Feb), select the external support model and mobilize partners/contractors (mid-Feb), secure seller agreement on the security/IAM approach (mid-Feb), receive seller user data and map to T-shirt sizes (mid-Feb), complete discovery and T-shirt sizing (Feb), finalize dual access design with InfoSec approval (Mar), pilot 50 users on dual access (mid-Mar), secure budget approval including external support costs (Apr).

Major Risks: (1) Delivery model decision - must select optimal external support option by mid-Feb to maintain timeline. (2) Seller security/IAM agreement - programme blocker if not resolved by late-Feb. (3) Internal capability gap - confirmed lack of skills/capacity requires significant external support. (4) Users on seller devices throughout 2026 - dependent on TSA service levels until physical migration complete.

Target Completion: InfoSec approval of dual access by 6-Mar-2026. Dual access pilot deployment Late-Mar-2026 (50 users). Remaining 430 users deployed within 2-3 weeks (Apr-2026). All 480 users on dual access by End-Apr-2026. Physical hardware migration Sep-Nov 2026 (3 waves: 150, 150, 180 users). All users migrated to corporate hardware by Nov-2026. TSA fully exited by End-Jan-2027. Programme closeout by End-Jan-2027.
